    Açaí bowl questions

    Before you order

    What comes in every açaí bowl?

    Every bowl comes with your base of choice and granola. Signature bowls come fully topped. Build your own bowls include two fruit toppings and one drizzle.

    Can I swap the base on a signature bowl?

    Yes. Each signature bowl has a suggested base, but you can pick any base you like.

    What is a split base bowl?

    Two bases side by side in one bowl, like açaí with mango or dragon fruit with passion fruit. Split base signature bowls are $12.99 and build your own split bowls are $11.99.

    Are the bowls made to order?

    Yes. Every bowl is built when you order it, with fresh fruit cut for the day.

    Ready for a bowl?

    Pickup orders go straight to the counter inside Animal Paradise Gyms. Delivery runs through the apps you already use.
