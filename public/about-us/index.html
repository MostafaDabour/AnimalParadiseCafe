About Animal Cafe

Fueling Vineland, one bowl at a time

A locally owned cafe inside Animal Paradise Gyms, making fresh food for people who train and people who just want something good.

Our story

More than a quick bite

Animal Cafe started with a simple idea: the people training at Animal Paradise Gyms deserved food as serious as their workouts. So we put a cafe inside the gym and stocked it with real fruit, quality protein and nothing that sits around waiting.

Today we are the go-to spot in Vineland for gym-goers, early commuters, students and anyone who wants a fresh açaí bowl or smoothie without the drive. We open at 4AM for the first sessions of the day and stay open until midnight for the last.
