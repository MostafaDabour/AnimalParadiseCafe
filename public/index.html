Quick answers

Do I need a gym membership to order?

No. Animal Cafe is open to everyone. Walk in through Animal Paradise Gyms, order at the counter, and you are good to go.

What are your hours?

Every day from 4AM to midnight, the same hours as the gym.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Bowls can be ordered ahead on our online pickup menu. Smoothies and protein shakes are made at the counter.

Do gym members get a discount?

Animal Paradise Gold members save 5% and Premium members save 10% at the cafe. See gym memberships.
